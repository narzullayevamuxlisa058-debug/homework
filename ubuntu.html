Ubuntu is a highly popular, open-source Linux operating system based on the Debian distribution and developed by Canonical Ltd. Renowned for its stability, robust security, and user-friendly interface, it serves as an excellent choice for personal computers, enterprise servers, cloud computing, and software developers alike. The system is completely free to use, comes pre-installed with a wide range of essential software, and follows a strict, predictable release schedule with long-term support (LTS) versions. Its name stems from a South African philosophy meaning "humanity towards others" or "I am because we are," reflecting the community-driven nature of its global ecosystem.
